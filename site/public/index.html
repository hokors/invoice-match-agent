Invoice Match Agent evaluation results

AI agent · Odoo 19 ERP · Gemini

An AI agent that catches suppliers overcharging, before anyone pays.

It reads supplier invoices (PDFs, office scans, phone photos), checks every line against what was ordered and what actually arrived, explains each problem, and files a draft bill in Odoo for a person to approve.

How it works

AI does the reading. Plain code does every money check. A person makes the final call.
